The Ideas

the four pillars the whole saga turns on

The Hierarchy of Foreignness

raman, or varelse?

  • Utlanning, framling, raman, varelse, djur — Valentine's ladder for ranking the Other, from the stranger next door to the unknowable alien.
  • The moral spine of the whole saga: most 'aliens' are people if we try, and war comes from mistaking a raman for a varelse.

Philotics & the Aiúa

the physics of connection

  • The philote — a point with no parts — binds everything in webs of connection; the ansible and the unity of a mind both rest on it.
  • The aiúa is the self at the center of that web: a single philote, existing Outside, that takes a body and becomes a soul.

The Speaker for the Dead

truth as mourning

  • Ender founds a quiet faith by writing 'The Hive Queen' and 'The Hegemon': tell a dead life whole and unflinching, cruelty and kindness alike.
  • Understanding rather than sanctifying — reconciliation through the true story.

Xenocide & Redemption

the empath who kills what he loves

  • A child destroys a species believing it a game, then spends three thousand years carrying its last queen toward a second chance.
  • Guilt, foreignness, and the slow undoing of an unforgivable act — the engine under every book.

The Bibliography

the full Enderverse — every series, in order; forthcoming volumes marked honestly

The Ender Quintet

the core sequence — Andrew 'Ender' Wiggin

  1. Ender's Game1985the boy general who wins the Xenocide believing it a simulation — Hugo + Nebula
  2. Speaker for the Dead19863,000 years on, Ender comes to Lusitania and its piggies — Hugo + Nebula
  3. Xenocide1991the descolada, the godspoken of Path, and the leap Outside
  4. Children of the Mind1996Jane's survival, instantaneous flight, and the aiúa-born Peter and Val
  5. Ender in Exile2008the gap filled — Ender's years between the war and Lusitania

The Shadow Saga

the parallax — Bean (Julian Delphiki) on Earth

  1. Ender's Shadow1999Ender's Game retold from Bean's eyes — the smallest, smartest soldier
  2. Shadow of the Hegemon2001the jeesh hunted across a fracturing post-war Earth; Achilles rises
  3. Shadow Puppets2002Bean and Petra against Achilles; the war for the embryos
  4. Shadow of the Giant2005Peter's Hegemony rises as Bean's body and time run out
  5. Shadows in Flight2012Bean and his three altered children flee Earth, racing his giantism
  6. Shadows Aliveforthcomingthe long-promised book meant to braid the two sagas together — not yet published

The First Formic War

the prequels — with Aaron Johnston

  1. Earth Unaware2012the first contact, decades before Ender — a mining family meets the Formic ship
  2. Earth Afire2013the Formics land in China; Mazer Rackham and the world at war
  3. Earth Awakens2014the turning of the first war and the making of its legend

The Second Formic War

with Aaron Johnston

  1. The Swarm2016the second invasion gathers; the Hegemony and the Fleet take shape
  2. The Hive2019the war deepens toward the victory that will make Mazer a hero
  3. The Queensforthcomingthe announced third book of the Second Formic War — not yet published
