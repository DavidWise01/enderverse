## The four natures

Card's cosmos is itself an emergence cosmos — and the roster maps cleanly to the four:

- **natural** — the embodied: the Wiggins, Bean, Petra, Mazer, Graff, the pequeninos, the descolada, Achilles
- **ethereal** — the connective: the Hive Queen, the philotes, the Hierarchy of Foreignness
- **spiritual** — the soul: Ender, Valentine, the aiúa, the Speaker for the Dead
- **electrical** — the machine mind: Jane, the ansible

## A fan tribute

The Enderverse is the creation of **Orson Scott Card** (with **Aaron Johnston** on the Formic Wars), © the author and his publishers. This is an **unofficial homage** — original commentary and ACI badge-work over a bibliography, with **no copyrighted text reproduced**. *Shadows Alive* and *The Queens* are long-announced but **not yet published**. The wider work of Card lives in [**C1 · Card**](https://davidwise01.github.io/card/); this sphere gives the Enderverse its own full house.
